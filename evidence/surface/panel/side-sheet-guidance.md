## Captured guidance

- Side sheets use a fixed-width presentation model.
- Side sheets typically span the full available height.
- Side-sheet dimensions depend on how the layout is divided into regions.
- The preferred edge is usually `end/right` to avoid conflict with left-edge navigation.
- A slight `16dp` inset may be used as an optional variant.
