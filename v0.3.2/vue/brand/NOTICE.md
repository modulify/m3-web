# Vue artwork

The Vue mark in `logo.svg` and `logo.png` was designed by Evan You. Source:
[Vue artwork repository](https://github.com/vuejs/art). The artwork is licensed
under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
with the additional usage conditions in that repository's README. It has been
resized, lowered by one eighth of its height, and placed in a transparent
circular cutout at the lower right of the Modulify M3 mark.

These adapted logo assets are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The package's ISC
